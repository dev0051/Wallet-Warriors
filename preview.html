The Problem

India's Credit Gap Is a Structural Failure

India's credit infrastructure is built for repeat borrowers. It systematically penalises first-timers, gig workers, students and freelancers — precisely the segment driving the next wave of economic growth.

Problem 01
The Thin File Borrower
Over 190 million Indians have no CIBIL score. Not because they're unreliable — because the system was never designed to see them.
Problem 02
Applying Blind
First-time borrowers don't know their approval probability, which lender to approach, or what to fix. Every rejection triggers a hard enquiry and drops their score further.
"A student who pays electricity on time for 2 years is demonstrating the same reliability as someone with a credit card. We just needed a way to prove it."
— The core insight behind Wallet Warriors

Core Features

Everything in One Intelligence Engine

🔬
Income Triangulation Engine
Cross-references declared income against electricity bills, UPI outflows & document proof. Flags incoherence with a Confidence Index score.
🤖
XGBoost Credit Model
Gradient-boosted classifier (AUC 0.86) produces a default probability and Approved/Rejected verdict with full SHAP explainability.
🕵️
Thin File Engine
No CIBIL? No problem. Reconstructs creditworthiness from bill payment history, UPI consistency, skill signals & savings discipline.
🎯
Approval Readiness Score
6-pillar composite score benchmarked against RBI Fair Lending guidelines. Shows exactly where you stand and what to fix.
🔎
SHAP Explainability
Waterfall charts, bar charts & feature tables show the exact contribution of every factor — turning a black-box into a transparent one.
🔮
What-If Simulator
Adjust savings, reduce expenses, boost CIBIL — see how your Approval Readiness changes in real time before committing to any action.
📄
Regulatory-Compliant PDF
Downloadable credit intelligence report compliant with RBI guidelines, SEBI disclosure standards & DPDP Act 2023.
🎓
Hustle Academy
3 in-app financial literacy modules: DTI mastery, UPI consistency signals, and the Credit Invisible → Bankable in 90 Days ladder.

Regulatory Compliance

Built to the Standard of Real FinTech

Every generated report is structured against three real Indian regulatory frameworks — not as a checkbox, but as a demo of what production deployment would require.

✓ RBI Fair Lending Guidelines ✓ SEBI Disclosure Standards ✓ DPDP Act 2023

Reports include a formal Data Usage Consent Notice (DPDP Sections 6, 7 & 13), RBI benchmark columns in the Approval Readiness table, SEBI non-advice disclaimers, a unique Report ID, and five separate regulatory disclaimer blocks.
